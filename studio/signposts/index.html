The idea

Booklet B questions come in a small number of shapes. The topic changes every time; the shape does not. A question about evaporation, a question about magnets and a question about food chains can all be the same shape, and that shape decides what the mark scheme is looking for.

So the first move on any open-ended question is not to think about the science. It is to ask which shape is this? The words in the stem tell you, if you know what to look for. Those words are the signposts.
