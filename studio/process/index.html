Process Skills

Variables, fair tests, and reading the data an examiner gives you.

Why this matters

A large share of Booklet B is not about recalling facts at all. It hands you an experiment and asks what was changed, what was measured, what should have been kept the same, why a setup is not a fair test, or which two rows of a table prove a point. These are learnable moves, and once you see the pattern they repeat across every topic.
