Stimulus-Based Conversation

In the real Paper 4, the examiner shows you a photograph and asks three questions on it. You speak; you do not write. So here you speak too. Pick a stimulus, read the scene, then hit Record and answer out loud. The examiner listens to the recording, writes down what it actually heard, and marks the spoken answer. Typing is still there as a fallback if you have no microphone.

No Gemini API key yet. Recording, playback, typing and the model answers all still work — only the AI marking and the stimulus generator need a key. Add one from Settings in the top right.
