Read it aloud. Hear it back. Get marked.

From 2025, Reading Aloud is worth 15 marks, and every passage comes with a given situation printed above it — who you are, who you are speaking to, and why. The extra marks are there to catch students who ignore it and read everything in the same school voice. So: read the situation, read the passage, record yourself, and let the examiner tell you what it actually heard.

No AI key set. Recording, playback, pace, cues, self-scoring and your history all work without one. Only the AI examiner needs a key — add it from the settings button at the top of this page.

Examiner model:

The examiner's marks

These marks come from an AI listening to your recording against a house rubric written for this app. SEAB does not publish band descriptors for Reading Aloud, so this is practice feedback, not a real exam mark.

Your own marking

Score yourself before you press Mark. Learning to hear your own reading is the skill that survives into the exam hall, where no one marks you out loud.

Your last takes

The strongest drill on this page: read one passage twice under two different given situations. If the two recordings sound the same, the tone marks are not being earned yet.
