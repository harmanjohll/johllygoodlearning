Paper 3: Listening Comprehension

Twenty items, three or four options each, seven texts, and every text is read twice. The questions are never read aloud, so you read them in the pause before the audio starts. Practice mode marks you item by item. Full Paper mode gives you nothing until you submit, which is what the real hall feels like.

20 marks ~35 min 10% of EL items 1–7 use pictures 2 readings, no rewind

Voice

Pick a voice. Different accents are worth rotating through, because the exam recording will not sound like your teacher.
