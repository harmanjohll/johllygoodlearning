Semakan Sistem

Empat semakan. Jalankannya sekali pada setiap peranti yang anda gunakan untuk belajar. Tiada apa-apa dihantar ke mana-mana kecuali dua ujian yang memang memanggil Google, dan kunci API anda tidak pernah meninggalkan pelayar ini.

Belum dijalankan

Tekan Jalankan semua semakan di bawah. Ujian mikrofon akan meminta kebenaran — itu memang dijangka.

Semakan 1

Pelayar dan sambungan

belum
Recording needs a secure connection and a browser that supports capture. This check also reports which audio format your browser will produce, because that differs between Chrome and Safari.
Semakan 2

Mikrofon

belum
Records three seconds and plays it back. Watch the bar move while you speak — if it stays flat, your device is sending a different microphone than you think.
Semakan 3

Kunci API

belum
Sends one tiny request to Google to confirm your saved key is accepted. If you have not added a key yet, use the settings button at the top of any page.
Semakan 4

Pemeriksa boleh mendengar anda

belum
The one that matters. Sends your three-second recording to Gemini and asks it to transcribe it. If the transcript matches roughly what you said, every Oral mode in the studio will work. Run check 2 first.
